    AKK Shop – Multi-Vendor Marketplace Platform

    UI/UX Design & Full-Stack Development · Ongoing Project

    Project Overview

    AKK Shop is a multi-vendor e-commerce marketplace designed to connect customers and independent sellers through one unified platform. The project covers the complete shopping journey, from discovering products and managing a shopping cart to checkout, order history, and delivery tracking. It also includes dedicated management interfaces for vendors and system administrators.

    The platform has been developed with a focus on clear user experiences, structured data management, and practical business workflows. Its architecture supports separate customer, vendor, and administrator journeys, with tools to manage stores, products, inventory, orders, payments, shipping information, and website content.

    System Features

    Customer Experience & Shopping

    • Customer registration, login, logout, profile editing, and delivery address management.
    • Marketplace homepage, product catalogue, categories, search results, store profiles, and detailed product pages.
    • Shopping cart management and cart validation before checkout.
    • Checkout summary, order confirmation, and shipping cost calculation using flat rates per order or per item.
    • Customer order history, order details, and shipment tracking.
    • Viva payment integration with payment responses returned to the application; exception flows are undergoing further testing.

    Vendor & Store Management

    • Seller registration, store applications, and application status tracking.
    • Vendor dashboard, store profile editing, shipping preferences, and payment information settings.
    • Product category management, product creation and editing, and product image management.
    • Inventory and stock management.
    • Seller order management, order processing, cancellations, and shipment/tracking record management.

    Administration & Platform Management

    • Administrative sign-in and dashboard for marketplace-wide operations.
    • Customer and vendor management, including review of new seller applications.
    • Centralised product, category, order, payment, and shipping management.
    • Vendor payment settings and configurable website information.
    • In-system notifications for orders, payments, and shipping, plus Terms, Privacy, and Returns & Refunds policy pages.
