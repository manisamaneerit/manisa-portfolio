Hotel Management & Booking System (Web Application)

development - 2025
DEMO FOR BOOKING

This project is a hotel management and booking system through a website that I designed and developed myself, from designing the work structure, features, and including designing the entire system database.

Hotel management system with room management system, billing system, receipt generation system, reporting tools and invoices, along with online room reservation system. Customers can book rooms directly through the website and connect to the automatic POS system.

System Features

  • Room management system with real-time room status tracking
  • Full-featured POS system for billing, service fees, check-in/check-out, and merchandise sales
  • Online room booking with customer self-service booking system
  • Automatic synchronization between online bookings and POS activities
  • Dashboard reporting and business analytics
  • Invoicing for all hotel transactions
  • Receipts for customer payments
  • In-house inventory and stock management

Technologies Used

  • Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, jQuery, Bootstrap
  • Backend: PHP, AJAX, JSON
  • Database: MySQL / MySQLi
